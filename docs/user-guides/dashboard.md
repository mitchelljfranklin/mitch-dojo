# Dashboard

The dashboard (`/dashboard`) is the landing page after login — a short, action-oriented worklist rather than a metrics wall.

## What's on it

| Widget | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Overdue payments** | Open invoices past their due date, newest first — your collections worklist |
| **Failed emails** | A chip appears when recent sends failed, linking to the [Email Log](../configuration/email-log) |
| **Getting-started** | Setup prompts until your dojo has data |

For the school's numbers — attendance trend, revenue, retention, members per location — open **Analytics** (see [Analytics](./analytics)).
